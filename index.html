About the temple

Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir is a Hindu temple dedicated to Shri Ram, in the historic city of Ayodhya. It is a place of worship and pilgrimage for devotees.

Significance

Ayodhya holds deep religious and cultural importance in Hindu tradition. Shri Ram is revered by Hindus as an embodiment of virtue and dharma.

What visitors may find meaningful

  • A place for prayer and devotion
  • A connection to the religious traditions of Ayodhya
  • Traditional temple architecture and craftsmanship

Visitor information

Entry guidance, darshan arrangements, schedules, and visitor rules may change. Check current details through official Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra communications before travelling.

Before travelling

  1. Confirm current timings and entry guidance through official communications.
  2. Follow instructions from temple staff and local authorities.
  3. Be considerate of worshippers and other visitors.
